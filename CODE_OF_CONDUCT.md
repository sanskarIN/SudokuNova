# SudokuNova Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SudokuNova is an open-source project intended to be welcoming, respectful, safe, and useful for contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

We commit to making participation free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, designs, and outcomes rather than people.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions patiently.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, quality, security, and release readiness.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Use inclusive and professional language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private contact details or credentials.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, secrets, or deceptive behavior.
- Encouraging public disclosure of an unpatched exploitable vulnerability.
- Repeated spam, donation pressure, or manipulative promotion.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, documentation collaboration, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and protect the safety and health of the community.
